About Praxis

Knowing is cheap. Explaining is the test.

Most of us consume ideas faster than we can use them. Praxis slows that down. Each spin gives you one idea: a bias, a fallacy, a myth, a strategy, a principle. You then do the one thing that proves you understand it, which is to explain it in your own words and apply it to your own life.

Go impromptu with a few seconds to gather your thoughts, or research with time to read the sources. Then respond by speaking aloud or by writing against the clock.

Praxis is Greek for action taken in light of understanding. The goal is fewer ideas, held more deeply.
